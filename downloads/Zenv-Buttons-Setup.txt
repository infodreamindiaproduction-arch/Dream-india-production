ZENV — ADD TO CART + BUY NOW UPDATE

1. Upload Zenv-Shopify-Theme-v4.zip in Shopify > Online Store > Themes > Import theme.
2. Preview the new theme, then publish it when satisfied. Existing product CSV alone does not update theme buttons.
3. Imported demo CSV entries have ZERO stock. In Shopify > Products > Inventory set the real available quantity for each sellable product and check availability on the Online Store channel.

Available default-variant product cards now show Add to Cart and Buy Now.
Products with multiple variants link to the product page so the buyer can select the correct option.
Product pages have both buttons; a selected unavailable variant disables purchase.
Out-of-stock products show disabled Add to Cart and Buy Now with an out-of-stock notice.
Unimported sample concepts show disabled purchase buttons until a real catalogue product exists.
Do not invent real inventory just to activate checkout.

Add to Cart uses the Shopify cart API, with native form fallback when JavaScript is unavailable.
Buy Now uses the native product form and return_to checkout, respecting the selected variant and quantity.
Standalone preview has simulated cart and simulated Buy Now; no payments/orders are submitted.
Theme Check and local browser checks were run. Live Shopify checkout needs verification in your store.
