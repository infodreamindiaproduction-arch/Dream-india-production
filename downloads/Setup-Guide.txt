WHAT IS ALREADY INCLUDED
Ten original Zenv demo concepts with compact square images: lipstick, gloss, foundation, blush,
kajal, mascara, six-colour eyeshadow palette, highlighter, primer and a five-piece brush set.
Demo INR prices range from 249 to 999. Each product has a description and suggested usage copy.
No copied reference-store products, fabricated reviews or fabricated discount comparisons are used.
All ten sample images and the demo home catalogue are inside the theme ZIP.
The preview has category filters, descriptions, search and a simulated add/remove cart.

SHOPIFY INSTALLATION
1. Upload Zenv-Shopify-Theme-v3.zip through Online Store > Themes > Import theme.
2. Open Customize / Preview. The ten sample product cards appear automatically until you select a real collection.
3. To create actual Shopify catalogue entries, use Products > Import > Zenv-10-Products.csv.
   Theme ZIP installation alone does not create Shopify product records.
4. The CSV creates ACTIVE sample entries with ZERO stock and deny-overselling, so they show Sold out.
   Check product availability on the Online Store sales channel after import.
   Sample products are for design preview; replace them with your real products before selling.
5. Keep the demo handles unchanged while using the sample images. For these ten handles, this theme
   automatically displays the bundled PNG if no real Shopify product image has been uploaded.
   This works on homepage cards, collection cards, product pages and cart fallback images.
6. Image Src is intentionally blank in the CSV: Shopify CSV cannot import images from a local ZIP or
   filesystem path. The included theme supplies the fallback images, so public image hosting is not needed.
   Shopify Admin thumbnails and other themes will be blank until you upload product media manually.
   To fill Admin product media, unzip Zenv-10-Product-Images.zip and upload the matching PNG to each product.
7. Make a collection containing tag zenv-demo, then choose it in Customize > Featured collection if desired.
   Default product-count setting is ten. Selecting your own collection replaces the sample home catalogue.

REPLACING DEMOS WITH YOUR PRODUCTS
Products > open each sample > edit title, description, price, photos, variants and inventory.
Or create new products and choose their collection in the theme editor, then delete the demo entries.
Real product images always take precedence over bundled sample imagery.
You can also remove the entire sample catalogue by replacing/removing the Featured collection home section.
The theme does not seed products or keep re-creating deleted catalogue entries.

BRANDING / NAVIGATION
Customize > Header: upload logo and choose your main menu.
Customize > Shop by category: set category photos and collection links.
Customize > Campaign hero, Beauty edits, Zenv story and Footer: change images, content and links.
Theme settings > Brand colours: change accent/background colours.
Create your real collections, About and Contact pages, and shipping/refund/privacy/terms policies.
Default collection handles and other install details are listed in the original setup guide if needed.

IMPORTANT PREVIEW DISTINCTIONS
Standalone preview cart is simulated and does not submit orders or collect payments.
A local preview may start with an empty bag; reload on the same browser retains its sample cart.
Theme sample cards before CSV import show descriptions, not working Add to cart for non-existent products.
Imported live catalogue products use normal Shopify product, inventory and checkout flows.
AI imagery, copy and prices are creative samples, not verified product formulations or manufacturer claims.
Live Shopify import, rendering, stock and checkout require your store and have not been executed here.
Before launch, replace sample data, set store currency INR, configure payments/shipping/taxes and test an order.
