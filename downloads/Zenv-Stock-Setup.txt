ZENV — 100 DEMO UNITS PER PRODUCT

All ten sample products in this CSV have quantity 100, inventory tracked by Shopify, overselling denied, status active and Published TRUE. Image URLs are populated. These are user-requested DEMO quantities, not verified physical inventory.

1. Update the theme using Zenv-Shopify-Theme-v4.zip for Add to Cart and Buy Now.
2. Extract Zenv-Products-Stock-100.zip and import its CSV under Shopify > Products > Import.
3. If these same demo handles already exist, enable the overwrite matching handles option. The CSV replaces their sample fields, so keep any real edits you want to preserve before importing.
4. Check product availability on the Online Store sales channel.
5. Shopify product CSV quantity applies to single-location stores. If your store has multiple locations, or existing quantities are not updated by product import, use Products > Inventory, select the correct location and set the ten demo products' available quantities to 100 there. The theme cannot change Shopify inventory or bypass unavailable stock.
6. Replace these sample quantities with real available stock before selling real products.

Buttons remain disabled if Shopify reports the selected variant unavailable. Preview checkout is simulated; actual checkout requires your store payment and shipping setup.
